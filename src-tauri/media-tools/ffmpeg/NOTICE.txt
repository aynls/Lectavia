Lectavia runs FFmpeg and FFprobe as separate programs.

Lectavia bundles LGPL builds of FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org/) with the
libmp3lame encoder (https://lame.sourceforge.io/). See dist/SOURCE.txt
for the specific build, its corresponding source, and its build recipe.
The upstream license notices are included in dist/LICENSE.txt (Windows and
Linux) or dist/LICENSES (macOS).

FFmpeg includes code based on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.
